## Universal Objects

### Universe

A universe is a large cluster of matter and space-time. Leaving the universe has similarities to leaving a cell.

### $\alpha$-Universe

A larger "universe" that have clusters of smaller "universes". For $0$-universe, it is a multiverse containing several universes and far more stable space-time, making the gravity from normal universes have an even smaller strength. For $\alpha+1$-universe, it contains several $\alpha$-universes. For limit ordinal $\alpha$, an $\alpha$-universe contains $\alpha_1$-universe, $\alpha_2$-universe, ... where $\sup\{\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\dots\}=\alpha$. For leaving an $\alpha$-universe, it requires at least $10\{\alpha\}10$ joules of energy.
