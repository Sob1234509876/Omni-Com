## A. Ordinals and the googology library

- [x] **1. BMS ordinals.** Add BMS ordinals into `:projects:oc:oc-googology`, note that for every standard form BMS formula, the ordinal it represents can be compared using lexicographical order. For example, BMS formula $A=BMS(0)(1,1)$ and $B=BMS(0)(1)(2)$ are all standard form, and we can find out $A>B$ because the second column of $A$ is $(1,1)$ and the second column of $B$ is $(1)$, which means $(1,1)>(1)$ thus $A>B$. But do note, $B=BMS(0)(1)(2)=BMS(0,0)(1,0)(2,0)=BMS(0,0,0)(1,0,0)(2,0,0)=$, for $C=BMS(0)(1,0)$ we have $(1,0)>(1)$ but it doesn't means $C>B$, instead we have $B>C$. — *implemented as the `bms` package: `BmsMatrix` keeps the canonical short form, `BmsOrdinal` compares standard forms by the reference's lexicographic rule (`BmsOrder`, including the trailing-zero convention the paragraph above warns about) and expands them by the bad-root rules. The article, `BmsOrdinal` and `BmsMatrix` are cross checked against Cantor normal form in the googology self check (698).*
- [x] **2. Non-recursive BMS.** Add Non Recursive BMS support for giant non recursive ordinals. The definition is over [here (Chinese GGG wiki link)](https://wiki.googology.top/index.php/%E9%9D%9E%E9%80%92%E5%BD%92BMS). Do remember to copy the page into this project and respect CC BY-SA 4.0. See [Ordinals.md](../Ordinals.md). — *`NonRecursiveBms` implements the second article's variant (a run of ones, a last `(1)` as successor, otherwise the BMS bad-root expansion); the article's infinite-tail case is exposed as the explicit `NonRecursiveExpansion` description because it is not a finite matrix, and comparisons reuse `BmsOrder`. The Chinese page and its CC BY-SA 4.0 notice live in `documents/AI/bms/` (`NOTICE.md`, the `.raw.wiki` and the captured HTML).*
- [x] **3. Abstract ordinals above BMS.** Add some abstract ordinals which can't be represented using BMS because of their sizes are way larger than the limit of BMS (SHO in CN GGG community or MHO in international GGG community). See [Ordinals.md](../Ordinals.md). — *the real specification is the 21-level ladder in `Ordinals.md` (notation functions `f(α)=TTBMS(0)(1^α)`, `TNRBMS`, the `ω_α` rungs, then the cardinal register up to `Ω_Absolute`), not the five-entry placeholder.
